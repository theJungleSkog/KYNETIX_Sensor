Upload Kynetix_XIAO_BLE_USB.ino, leave sensors unloaded for two seconds after power-on, then select Connect Bluetooth (Chrome or Edge). USB fallback is also available.
Connection & sensor setup XIAO nRF52840 / five FSR inputs
1 / Prepare the device

Upload Kynetix_XIAO_BLE_USB.ino using the Seeed nRF52 mbed-enabled core and ArduinoBLE library. The pins are {A0, A1, A2, A3, A4}. Power up with sensors unloaded for the first two seconds.

2 / Connect the stream

Use Chrome or Edge on a Bluetooth-equipped computer. Press Connect Bluetooth and choose Kynetix Insole. For USB fallback, close Serial Monitor and press Connect USB. If browser Bluetooth is blocked on a local file, serve this HTML from localhost.

3 / Verify sensor placement

S1 big toe (A0/D0) · S2 lateral midfoot (A1/D1) · S3 fifth metatarsal (A2/D2) · S4 first metatarsal (A3/D3) · S5 heel (A4/D4).

4 / Interpret correctly

Heat-map colors and channel bars reflect approximate baseline-adjusted sensor voltage (not calibrated pressure). GCT comes from the Arduino contact flag. Foot-strike and cadence are preliminary estimates, not medical advice.
